## The pitch

Send this to interested co-ops.

> A few of us are starting a group that finds gaps in the student experience in Brightspace and ships fixes. We are all current students, which is a perspective the product org can't easily get and which expires when we graduate.
>
> John Baker is sponsoring it, so it's sanctioned, but it's additive to normal co-op work and your sprint commitments come first. Two to four hours a week.
>
> Two things are in flight. A grade calculator sandbox on the student grades page, and better handling of code submissions for programming courses. Both came from being annoyed at Brightspace as a student.
>
> If you're interested, read the charter and come to the first meeting. Tell your manager before you commit, there's a script in the docs.

## Telling your manager

Before you commit, not after.

> I've been asked to join a co-op led group that proposes and builds student facing improvements to Brightspace. John Baker is sponsoring it. It's additional to my regular work, two to four hours a week, and the rule is sprint commitments come first. Wanted you to hear it from me and check you're okay with it.

If they say no or want it scaled back, that is the answer. No appeal to the sponsor.

## First meeting

45 minutes.

1. **Why this exists**, 5 min
2. **Charter and rules**, 10 min. Especially day job first, managers know, never route around a team.
3. **What is in flight**, 10 min. Walk both proposals and invite disagreement.
4. **Idea round**, 15 min. Everyone brings one thing that annoyed them about Brightspace as a student. Capture all of them, triage none yet.
5. **Logistics**, 5 min. Weekly slot, channel, who is in.

Do not assign work in the first meeting.

## Size

Four to six active members for the first term. Bigger produces meetings rather than code. Keep a waiting list.
