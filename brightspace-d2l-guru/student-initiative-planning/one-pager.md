# Brightspace Student Experience Initiative

One page. Fall 2026.

## What it is

D2L co-ops who are current university students, finding gaps in the student experience in Brightspace and turning them into shipped changes. Additive to our regular co-op work.

## Why it is worth doing

Every co-op here uses Brightspace at their own university. That perspective is hard for the product org to source and it expires when we graduate.

The gaps are visible from outside too. Students install browser extensions to get grade calculation. Instructors teaching programming move whole courses onto other platforms because code does not fit the generic assignment tools. That is demand the product is not capturing.

## How it works

1. Any co-op raises an idea
2. Weekly triage, most get dropped or parked
3. Survivors get written up with evidence, scope, and the strongest objection stated honestly
4. The team that owns the code reviews it before anyone else sees it
5. With their approval, we build it through the normal process

No shortcuts around design review, code review, accessibility review, or a PM's judgement.

## In flight

**Grade calculator sandbox.** Students enter hypothetical scores on ungraded items and see their projected final grade, or set a target and see what they need. Real grades stay locked, nothing saves, instructors see nothing. This is the shipping target.

**Code aware assignments.** Code submissions currently arrive as generic attachments with no highlighting, no project structure, and no way to comment on a line. This makes them render and annotate properly. No code execution involved.

## What we ask of leadership

- Sponsorship, so this reads as sanctioned rather than freelancing
- Introductions to the teams that own Grades and Assignments
- A monthly lunch, agenda ahead, recap after
- A decision when something is stuck

## What we ask of managers

Awareness and agreement. Two to four hours a week. Sprint commitments come first and anyone whose delivery is affected steps back.

## Success this term

1. One student facing change shipped or merged
2. Two proposals a PM can act on
3. A process the Winter 2027 cohort can continue without the current lead
