# Charter

## What we do

1. Find gaps in the student experience in Brightspace
2. Write them up so a product team can act on them
3. Build the ones we get approval to build

## In scope

- Student facing features and workflows
- Things a few co-ops can ship in one term
- Written proposals for bigger ideas a real team would own

## Out of scope

- Anything already on a roadmap
- Instructor or admin work, unless it is the only way to fix a student problem
- Redesigns based on taste rather than a named problem

## Rules

- **Day job first.** Sprint commitments win. If this affects your delivery, you step back, no penalty.
- **Managers know.** Nobody joins without telling their manager. Script in [joining.md](joining.md).
- **Never route around a team.** If an idea touches their code, they see it before anyone else does, including the CEO.
- **Sponsorship is air cover, not a bypass.** Same design review, code review, and accessibility review as any other change.
- **One shipped thing beats ten proposals.**

## Time

Two to four hours a week for members. More for the lead and during a build. If it is consistently more, the scope is wrong.

## What we ask of John

Air cover, introductions, and a decision when something is stuck. Not sign off on daily calls.

Get this confirmed in writing. Draft in [one-pager.md](one-pager.md).

## Success for Fall 2026

1. One student facing change shipped or merged
2. Two proposals a product manager can act on, one formally reviewed by the owning team
3. A process the Winter 2027 cohort can pick up without the current lead
4. Nobody's regular delivery affected

## Continuity

Terms are four months, so this has to survive turnover.

- Everything lives in the repo, not in DMs
- Last two weeks are handoff, using `templates/handoff-template.md`
- The lead names a successor before term end
