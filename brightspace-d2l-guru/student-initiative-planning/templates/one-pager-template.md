# One pager template

For leadership, managers, or any team seeing this for the first time. One page, not two.

## What it is

Two sentences.

## Why it is worth doing

The problem, and the evidence that it is real and not just your opinion. External evidence is strongest, since it does not rely on anyone trusting the group yet.

## How it works

The process, in a numbered list short enough to read standing up. Include the part about not routing around teams, because that is what a reader is quietly worrying about.

## What is in flight

One short paragraph per item. What it is, what it does for the user, and what state it is in.

## What we are asking for

Be specific. Sponsorship, an introduction, a decision, a slot on someone's calendar. Vague asks get vague answers.

## What we are asking of managers

Time commitment, and the guardrail that regular delivery comes first.

## What success looks like

Two or three criteria you could be held to.

## Lead

Name, role, team.
