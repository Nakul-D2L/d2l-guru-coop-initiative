# End of term handoff template

Written in the last two weeks of a co-op term. The point is that the next cohort does not restart from zero.

## Term summary

- **Term**
- **Lead**
- **Members**

## Shipped

What actually made it into the product, with links to the proposal and the merged work.

## In flight

What is part built or part approved, where exactly it stands, and what the next step is. Be specific enough that someone can pick it up without finding you.

## Stalled or dropped

What did not work and why. This is the most valuable section. Include anything a team told us was already planned, already tried, or not worth doing.

## Relationships

Who we worked with, on which team, and what they care about. Names matter more than process here.

## What I would do differently

Honest. One paragraph.

## For the next cohort

- Recommended first pickup
- What to leave alone
- Who to talk to in week one

## Incoming lead

Named before term end, with a handover conversation actually scheduled.
