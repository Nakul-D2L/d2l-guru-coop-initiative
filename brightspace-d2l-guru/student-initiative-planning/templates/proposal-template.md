## Problem

Two sentences. What is the student trying to do and what makes it hard today. No jargon.

## Evidence

Why this is real beyond one person's experience. Support tickets, community forum threads, third party extensions or workarounds, competitor behaviour, students you actually asked.

## Who it affects

Rough scale. All students, students in weighted courses, students on mobile, and so on.

## What exists today

How people solve this now, inside Brightspace and outside it. Include any partial support that already exists so we do not rebuild it. Fill this in before writing the proposed change.

## Proposed change

What we would build, specific enough that an engineer could disagree with details.

## Scope

- **In** the smallest version that solves the problem
- **Out** everything deliberately excluded from v1

## Technical approach

How it fits the existing architecture. Where the data comes from. What is client side and what is not.

## Acceptance criteria

Testable statements. If all of these are true, it works.

## Risks and objections

The strongest case against building this, written honestly. If you cannot think of one, you have not thought about it enough.

## Open questions

With a named person who can answer each.

## Effort and owner

Rough size, who would build it, and over what period.
