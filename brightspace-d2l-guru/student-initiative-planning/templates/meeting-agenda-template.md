# Meeting agenda template

## Weekly sync

30 minutes. Cancel it if there is nothing to decide.

**In flight**
- What moved since last week, one line each
- Anything blocked, and who can unblock it

**Triage**
- New ideas from the backlog. Drop, park, or write up.

**Actions**
- [ ] owner, what, by when

## Lunch with a sponsor or executive

Their time is the scarce resource. Bring decisions, not updates.

**Send the day before**

Three agenda items maximum. For each one, state plainly what you want from them, a decision, an introduction, or a green light. Attach at most one page of reading.

1. **[Item]** — what I want from you is
2. **[Item]** — what I want from you is
3. **[Item]** — what I want from you is

**Send the same day, after**

- What was decided
- What I am doing next, with dates
- What you said you would do

Skipping the recap is how a good conversation turns into nothing three months later.

---

## Team review of a proposal

45 minutes with the team that owns the surface area.

1. Problem, two minutes, no solution yet
2. Evidence, three minutes
3. What already exists, and what we might have missed. Ask them, do not tell them.
4. Proposed change, ten minutes
5. Objections. Open with the strongest one against your own proposal.
6. What we are asking for. Usually a yes or no on whether this is worth pursuing, and who the product owner would be.

Go in expecting to be told it is already planned or already tried. That is a good outcome, not a failure.
